## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Withering Realms**. This trainer enhances exploration and combat in Withering Realms by providing configurable health, resource, equipment, crafting, and movement controls. It is designed around the game's world-map progression, randomized dungeon elements, doll-arm equipment, nighttime exploration, and action RPG combat.

**Target Audience:** Players who want to explore Penwyll more freely, test different doll-arm loadouts, reduce combat difficulty, experiment with crafting, or revisit randomized areas without repeating resource-grinding sections.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## ⚡ Features (v1.0)

### 🎯 Gameplay

- **Infinite Health** — Prevents Clover from losing health during exploration and combat.
- **Infinite Resources** — Keeps available gameplay resources from being depleted.
- **Free Crafting** — Allows weapons and machines to be crafted without consuming required materials.
- **Equipment Durability** — Prevents equipped weapons and machines from degrading during use.
- **Instant Crafting** — Removes waiting time from supported crafting operations.
- **Save Protection** — Reduces the risk of losing progress while experimenting with trainer options.

### 🧰 Inventory

- **Unlimited Currency** — Maintains currency for purchases from Penwyll shops and other vendors.
- **Inventory Protection** — Protects collected items, equipment, materials, and magical artifacts from being consumed or lost.

### ⚔️ Combat

- **Doll Arm Equipment** — Supports experimentation with craftable doll-arm equipment such as the Enchanted Candelabrum, Chain Rock, Machinegun, and Flamethrower.
- **Unlimited Ammunition** — Prevents ammunition and other ranged-weapon resources from being depleted.
- **One-Hit Defeat** — Greatly increases damage dealt to hostile creatures and perimeter guardians.
- **Damage Protection** — Reduces or disables incoming damage from enemies and environmental hazards.

### 🚶 Movement

- **Fast Movement** — Increases Clover's movement speed while exploring areas and dungeons.
- **Traversal Assistance** — Helps the player test exploration routes and revisit locations that require new traversal abilities.

### 🌍 World

- **Night Exploration Support** — Makes repeated nighttime visits to areas easier while searching for additional creatures, secrets, and treasure.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Activate Fast Movement before leaving a safe area. Use the option to cover large sections of the world map more quickly, inspect side paths, and revisit locations after gaining new traversal abilities. Disable it during precise navigation or scripted sequences if movement becomes difficult to control.

### Tools & Inventory

Enable Inventory Protection before collecting rare materials, equipment, or magical artifacts. Unlimited Currency is useful when purchasing supplies or experimenting with shop inventories in Penwyll. Free Crafting and Instant Crafting are best used together when testing different weapons and machines for the doll's missing right arm.

### World & Environment

The game uses a world map that expands as major perimeter guardians are defeated. Use movement and resource options to investigate tombs, caves, graveyards, and rotting estates at your own pace. Night Exploration Support is useful when returning to completed areas to find altered encounters, additional secrets, and new treasure.

### Combat & Weapons

Activate Damage Protection for safer exploration or use One-Hit Defeat when testing weapons against hostile creatures and large perimeter guardians. Unlimited Ammunition supports ranged equipment such as the Machinegun, while equipment durability protection allows extended testing of the Enchanted Candelabrum, Chain Rock, and Flamethrower.
